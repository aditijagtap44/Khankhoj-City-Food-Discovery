
# 🍽️ Khankhoj – City Food Discovery Platform
Discover Every City's Real Taste.

Khankhoj is a city-based food discovery platform designed to help **new residents, students, travelers, tourists, and local food lovers** discover the real food culture of a city.

Instead of simply listing restaurants, Khankhoj helps users understand **what a city is famous for, what food they should try, where they can find it, and which hidden local food places are worth discovering.**

---

## 🌟 Why Khankhoj?

When people arrive in a new city, finding good local food can be difficult. Search engines and restaurant platforms often provide large lists, but they may not explain the **food culture and local specialties** of a city.

Khankhoj organizes this information into simple **city food guides**, helping users discover:

* 🍛 Famous local dishes
* 🌮 Street food
* 🥘 Traditional food
* 🏪 Local food places
* 💎 Hidden food gems
* 💰 Budget-friendly options
* 📍 Food places by location
* ⭐ Ratings and reviews

### Our Goal

**Help people discover a city through its food.**

## ✨ Key Features

### 🏙️ City Food Guides

Explore food guides for different cities and discover their famous dishes, local specialties, street food, and hidden gems.

Discover the dishes a city is known for, with descriptions, food categories, price ranges, and recommended places.

### 💎 Hidden Food Gems

Find small local shops, street stalls, traditional eateries, and underrated food places that may be difficult to discover through generic searches.

### 🔍 Search & Filters

Search for cities, dishes, or food places and filter results by:

* Non-Vegetarian
* Street Food
* Restaurant
* Café
* Local Food
* Budget Friendly
* Premium
* Hidden Gem

### 📍 Food Place Discovery

View useful information about food places including:

* Location
* Specialty dishes
* Price range
* Opening hours
* Food category
* Reviews

### ⭐ Ratings & Reviews

Users can share their food experiences through ratings and reviews.

### ❤️ Favorites

Users can save food places they want to visit later.

### 📸 Community Contributions

Khankhoj is designed with an SEO-friendly content structure to help users discover city food guides through search engines.
